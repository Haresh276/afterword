STATEMENT OF WORK - Brand Identity for Lumen Coffee Roasters

Client: Daniel Becker (daniel@lumencoffee.example), Lumen Coffee Roasters GmbH, Berlin, Germany
Contract value (total): USD 2,400.00
Payment: via PayPal invoice per milestone, due within 7 days of invoice.

Milestone 1: Discovery & moodboards - USD 600 - due 2026-11-09
Acceptance criteria:
- Two moodboards delivered as PDF, each with at least 12 reference images
- One-page brand brief summarising audience, tone and competitors
- Delivered via shared Google Drive folder

Milestone 2: Logo design - USD 1,000 - due 2026-11-20
Acceptance criteria:
- Three distinct logo concepts in vector format (SVG and AI)
- Each concept shown in colour and monochrome
- Up to two rounds of revisions on the chosen concept

Milestone 3: Brand guidelines - USD 800 - due 2026-12-01
Acceptance criteria:
- Brand guidelines PDF of at least 12 pages
- Colour palette with HEX, RGB and CMYK values
- Typography system with primary and secondary typefaces
- Final logo files in SVG, PNG (transparent) and PDF

Intellectual property transfers to the Client on full payment of each milestone.
Revisions beyond those listed are billed at USD 40/hour.
